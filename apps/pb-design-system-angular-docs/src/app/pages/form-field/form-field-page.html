Every PB text field (pb-input, pb-select, pb-autocomplete, pb-datepicker, pb-timepicker) is wrapped in the M3 form field: floating label, fill or outline container, supporting and error text, and prefix/suffix content.

API reference

InputTypeDescription
labelstringThe floating label.
appearance'fill' | 'outline'The container style. Defaults to 'fill'.
supportingTextstringHint text under the field.
errorTextstringShown instead of the hint, and puts the field in its error state.
required, disabledbooleanRequired marker; disabled state.
blockbooleanStretches the field to the container's full width.
